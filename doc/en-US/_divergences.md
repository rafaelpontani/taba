| 18 | `04-cluster.md` (6.3, "Accepted risk") | The lack of a majority prevents exclusions, "future evolution: manual forced-exclusion route" | Verified: with 3 nodes, bringing down **a second node before the first returns** (1 survivor < `cluster.vote.min-voters`=2) leads to `quorum-lost`; the two restarted nodes **cannot rejoin** (`cluster.registration-failed`, the register requires all nodes of the table, including the dead one) and **there is no automatic recovery or manual route**. Way out found: bring everything down (`docker compose down`) and start again | Code; design limit. **PRB candidate (risk)** |
| 19 | `04-cluster.md` (6.10) | "The cluster does not change: every start is a new node" | Verified: `docker compose down` + `up` (volumes kept) recreates the cluster **with no topic and no messages**, including the topic definitions.
